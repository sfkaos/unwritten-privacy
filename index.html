Privacy Policy

Unwritten — Write to Unlock

Effective Date: March 25, 2026

Thank you for using Unwritten. Your privacy matters, and we want to be upfront: we do not collect any of your data. This policy explains what that means in practice.

Information We Collect

None. Unwritten does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers. There are no user accounts, no sign-ups, no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising.

Your writing content stays entirely on your device. We never see it, and neither does anyone else.

How Your Data Is Stored

All app data — including your writing entries, settings, and preferences — is stored locally on your device using on-device storage (MMKV). This data is never uploaded, synced, or backed up to any server we operate.

If you delete the app, all locally stored data is permanently removed.

Screen Time Permission

Unwritten uses Apple's FamilyControls framework to lock and unlock apps on your device. When you grant Screen Time authorization, the app uses this permission solely to:

  • Apply app restrictions (shields) at your chosen schedule
  • Remove those restrictions when you complete your daily writing session

This permission is used entirely on-device. No information about your Screen Time usage, restricted apps, or device activity is collected or transmitted anywhere.

Third-Party Services

Unwritten does not use any third-party SDKs, services, or APIs that collect data. The app makes no network requests. There is no analytics platform, no crash reporting service, and no advertising framework embedded in the app.

Children's Privacy

Unwritten is not directed at children under the age of 13. Because the app collects no data from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child is using the app in a way that concerns you, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this privacy policy, we will post the revised version here with a new effective date. Since the app makes no network requests, updates to this policy would accompany an app update.

Contact
