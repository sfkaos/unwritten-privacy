The Short Version

Here's what actually matters, before the formal stuff.

  • Your writing stays on your device. We cannot read your journal entries. They are stored locally and are never uploaded, unless you turn on AI Insights.

  • AI Insights is opt-in, always. If you enable it, your entry text is sent for analysis. It is off by default. You must turn it on yourself.

  • We collect usage analytics. Things like which screens you open and when you finish a writing session. They are tied to a random ID, not to your name or email. They never include your journal entries.

  • Error reports never include your writing. When the app or this website runs into an error, it can send us a short technical report: the kind of error and where in our code it happened. Never your writing, and never the text of the error message.

  • We don't sell your data. There are no ads inside Unwritten. We do advertise Unwritten on other platforms and measure whether those ads work. Details are in Section 2.

  • Deleting the app deletes what is on your phone. Removing Unwritten removes your journal and settings from your device permanently. Data already sent to the services in Section 6 is not removed by deleting the app. Email us and we will help.

1. Who We Are

Unwritten is a mobile journaling app that locks your phone until you complete a daily writing session.

This privacy policy applies to the Unwritten iOS app distributed through the Apple App Store and to this website, getunwritten.app.

Questions? Reach us at: privacy@getunwritten.app

2. What We Collect

What stays on your device

The following data never leaves your phone, except entry text you choose to send through AI Insights (Section 4):

  • Journal entries: your writing, word counts, and timestamps

  • App settings: your lock schedule, word goal, theme, reminder and notification preferences

  • Streak data: your writing streak history and milestones

  • Your saved writing prompts, and any AI Insights results returned to your device

This data is stored locally using on-device storage (MMKV). We have no access to it. If you delete the app, this data is permanently and immediately removed. Reminders are scheduled on your device as local notifications. We do not use a push notification service.

What goes to third-party services

Usage analytics (app)

We collect analytics events using PostHog (hosted in the United States). These include actions such as which onboarding steps you see and finish, starting, finishing, or leaving a writing session, word counts, session length, streak counts, settings you change, which plan you pick on the subscription screen, and whether a purchase went through. They also include the answers you pick during onboarding, such as how you usually feel in the morning and how you would rather feel. If you type your own answer to the onboarding question about what you want from Unwritten, that typed answer is included too.

PostHog's software also adds basic technical details to each event: app version, operating system version, device type and model, screen size, and language and time zone settings. PostHog receives your IP address when the app sends events and may use it to estimate an approximate location, such as country and city. Events are tied to a random ID that PostHog creates when you first open the app. We do not attach your name or email to them. Analytics events never include your journal entries, your saved prompts, or the text of any feedback you send. We use this data to understand how the app is used and to decide what to improve.

Error reports (app)

When the app runs into an unexpected error, it can send an error report to PostHog, the same service we use for analytics. Error reports are built from a fixed list of allowed fields, and anything outside that list is dropped before the report leaves your phone. A report contains only:

  • The kind of error, chosen from a fixed list (for example a network error or a type error)

  • A short category code in place of the error message. The message text itself is never sent.

  • Where in the app's code the error happened: the name of the app's code bundle file and a line and column number. No function names and no other code text.

  • App version and build number, operating system and version, device type, screen size, and whether it is a simulator

  • A random session ID, and whether the app caught the error or it went unhandled

Error reports never include your writing, prompt text, names, email addresses, or any other text you type. They are tied to the same random ID as analytics. We use them only to find and fix bugs.

We also give RevenueCat the random analytics ID described above, so subscription events such as a trial starting, a renewal, or a cancellation show up in our analytics. The app includes Meta's software development kit so we can tell whether our ads on Facebook and Instagram lead to installs and subscriptions. Meta's SDK automatically records app installs and app opens. The app also sends Meta events for finishing onboarding and for starting a trial or subscription, including the plan, its price, and the currency. RevenueCat may also report paid subscriptions to Meta. [CONFIRM: the RevenueCat to Meta integration is turned on.] It never sends your writing. If you choose "Allow" on Apple's tracking prompt, Meta also receives your device's advertising identifier so it can match your install to an ad you saw. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not shared.

Feedback you send us

If you send feedback from the app, we receive your message, the reply email address if you choose to give one, your app version and build, device model, iOS version, whether you are on the free plan or Unwritten+, how many writing sessions you have completed, and your random analytics ID. It passes through our server (Supabase) and is delivered to our inbox by email through Resend. Your journal entries are never included.

AI Writing Insights (opt-in only)

If you choose to enable AI Writing Insights, your journal entry text is transmitted to our server infrastructure for analysis. This feature is off by default. See Section 4 for full details.

What we never collect

4. AI-Powered Features

Unwritten offers an optional AI Writing Insights feature that analyzes your journal entries to identify emotions, generate brief summaries, and extract topics.

How it works

  1. You enable AI Writing Insights in Settings (off by default)

  2. Before activation, we show you a clear disclosure explaining what data is transmitted

  3. When you complete a writing session, your entry text is sent from your device to our server (hosted on Supabase, AWS US East) via HTTPS

  4. Our server forwards your text to OpenRouter, which routes it to Anthropic's Claude language model for analysis

  5. The AI returns a short analysis: 1 or 2 emotions, a brief summary, and 1 to 3 topics

  6. The analysis is sent back to your device and stored locally

  7. Your entry text is processed in memory. It is not permanently stored on any server.

Third-party AI providers

  • Supabase (supabase.com) hosts our server-side function that processes your request. Your entry text passes through Supabase infrastructure during analysis. Supabase's data processing agreement: supabase.com/legal/dpa

  • OpenRouter (openrouter.ai) routes your text to the AI model. Privacy policy: openrouter.ai/privacy

  • Anthropic (anthropic.com) provides the Claude language model that performs the analysis. Privacy policy: anthropic.com/privacy

Your controls

  • AI Writing Insights is off by default. You must explicitly enable it.

  • You can disable it at any time in Settings. Disabling it stops all future text transmission immediately.

  • The core app (writing, phone locking, journaling, streaks) works fully without AI Insights.

  • Your writing is not used to train AI models. OpenRouter is configured with zero data retention for requests made through Unwritten. [CONFIRM: the zero data retention setting is on in the OpenRouter account. It is not set in the app's code.]

5. Screen Time and Device Controls

Unwritten uses Apple's FamilyControls and ManagedSettings frameworks to apply and remove app restrictions on your device.

When you grant Screen Time authorization:

  • The app applies shields (restrictions) to other apps at your chosen schedule

  • The app removes those shields when you complete your daily writing session

  • All enforcement happens entirely on your device through Apple's system frameworks

  • The app uses opaque tokens provided by Apple. It never knows which specific apps are on your device or how you use them.

  • No Screen Time data, device activity data, or app usage data is collected or transmitted

You can revoke Screen Time authorization at any time through your device's Settings app.

7. Data Retention

  • On-device data: retained until you delete the app or clear app data. We have no access to this data.

  • Analytics data and error reports: kept in PostHog for [CONFIRM: PostHog event retention period for project 362063], then deleted.

8. Security

  • All data transmitted from the app uses HTTPS (TLS 1.2 or higher)

  • On-device data is stored using MMKV, which leverages iOS hardware encryption

  • Our server infrastructure (Supabase) uses AES-256 encryption at rest

  • AI Insights and feedback use anonymous authentication. No personally identifiable credentials are stored.

  • Server-side API keys are stored as encrypted secrets and are never embedded in the app

  • Error reports are assembled from a fixed list of allowed fields, and any report that does not match that list is dropped instead of sent

9. Your Rights and Choices

All users

  • Disable AI Writing Insights at any time in Settings

  • Delete the data on your phone by deleting the app

  • Choose "Ask App Not to Track" on Apple's tracking prompt, or change it later in your iPhone's Settings

  • Manage or cancel your subscription through Apple's subscription settings

  • Ask us to delete analytics data, feedback, or contact form messages by emailing privacy@getunwritten.app

Users in the European Economic Area (GDPR)

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict processing of, and port your personal data. You also have the right to withdraw consent for AI Insights processing at any time and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Because your journal data is stored only on your device, most of these rights are exercised directly through the app. For analytics data, error reports, feedback, or contact form messages, contact us at privacy@getunwritten.app.

Users in California (CCPA/CPRA)

You have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request deletion of your personal information, and to opt out of the sale of personal information.

We do not sell personal information. To exercise your rights, contact us at privacy@getunwritten.app.

10. Children's Privacy

Unwritten is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete that information promptly. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at privacy@getunwritten.app.
